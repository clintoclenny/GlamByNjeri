About Glam By Njeri

Glam By Njeri is a premier beauty service provider based in Kitengela, offering top-quality nail, hair, and face treatments. Our mission is to make every client feel confident, elegant, and pampered. With a passion for beauty and a dedication to excellence, we bring personalized services to your doorstep or at our studio.

Founded by Njeri, our team of trained professionals uses the latest techniques and premium products to ensure you leave looking and feeling fabulous. Whether it is a casual makeover or a special event, Glam By Njeri is your trusted beauty partner.
